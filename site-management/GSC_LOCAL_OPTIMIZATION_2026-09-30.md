## Change strategy

For this batch:

- align titles and first-screen language more closely with the query cluster;
- preserve one H1 per page;
- add concise first-party Quick Answer blocks;
- preserve existing factual service claims rather than inventing neighborhood or travel facts;
- retain descriptive links back to the relevant service hubs and adjacent first-party resources;
- update sitemap `lastmod` for materially changed pages;
- avoid further changes to the homepage, `/services/`, `/office-cleaning/`, and `/locations/` until post-release data can accumulate.

The relevant service hubs already contain descriptive links to these local pages, so this batch does not rewrite those hubs merely to create additional link churn.
