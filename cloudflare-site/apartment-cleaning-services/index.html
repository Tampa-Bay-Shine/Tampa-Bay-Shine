Apartment & Condo Logistics

Help Us Prepare for Building Access

Apartment and condo appointments can involve parking, elevators, controlled entrances or building policies. Providing the details ahead of time helps the cleaning appointment run smoothly.

Access Instructions

  • Gate or building-entry codes
  • Concierge or leasing-office procedures
  • Lockbox or key instructions
  • Building access windows

Parking & Elevators

  • Guest or garage parking instructions
  • Loading-zone information
  • Service-elevator requirements
  • Elevator reservations when required

Pets & Property Notes

  • Pet information
  • Special cleaning priorities
  • Sensitive surfaces
  • Areas that should not be entered

Apartment Cleaning Questions

Apartment & Condo Cleaning FAQs

How much does apartment cleaning cost?
Standard Cleaning starts at $114 for the lowest one-time configuration. Deep Cleaning starts at $199, and Move-In / Move-Out Cleaning starts at $259. Your exact price depends on the property and selected service options and is shown online before you enter payment information.
What’s included in Standard Apartment Cleaning?
Standard Cleaning covers routine cleaning of kitchens, bathrooms, bedrooms and common living areas, including surface wiping, light dusting, vacuuming, mopping and trash removal. Available extras can be selected during booking.
Do you offer recurring apartment cleaning?
Yes. Eligible recurring Standard Cleaning can be scheduled weekly, every other week, every 3 weeks or every 4 weeks.
Do you bring cleaning supplies?
Yes. Tampa Bay Shine brings cleaning supplies for the scheduled service. Customer-provided products or equipment can also be used when requested.
Can you coordinate with my concierge or apartment building?
Yes. Provide parking, elevator, gate, concierge, leasing-office and building-access instructions when booking so the cleaning professional can prepare for the appointment.
Do you offer apartment move-out cleaning?
Yes. Apartment Move-Out Cleaning is designed for vacant or substantially empty apartments being prepared for inspection, lease turnover or property handoff.
Will I usually have the same cleaner for recurring apartment service?
Tampa Bay Shine generally keeps the same cleaning professional on recurring schedules whenever availability allows. Another background-checked cleaning professional may service the appointment when needed.
When is my card charged?
Your card is typically pre-authorized 1–2 days before the scheduled cleaning and charged after the cleaning is completed.
Which apartment cleaning areas have dedicated pages?
Tampa Bay Shine currently has dedicated apartment cleaning pages for Tampa, Brandon, Riverview, Apollo Beach and Wesley Chapel. Service is also available in additional Tampa Bay communities depending on scheduling availability.
