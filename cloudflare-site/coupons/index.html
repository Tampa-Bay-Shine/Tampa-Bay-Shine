Coupon Questions

Tampa Bay Shine Cleaning Coupon FAQs

How do I use coupon code RECUR30?
Choose an eligible weekly, every-other-week, every-3-weeks, or every-4-weeks recurring residential cleaning schedule during online booking and enter RECUR30. Eligible new customers receive 30% off the first recurring cleaning.
What are the requirements for RECUR30?
RECUR30 is for new customers and requires a minimum $100 service value before discount. The customer must complete three total cleanings: the discounted first cleaning plus two additional recurring cleanings. If recurring service is canceled before all three are completed, the original 30% introductory discount will be charged back.
Does RECUR30 stack with my recurring discount?
No. RECUR30 replaces the normal recurring-frequency discount on the first cleaning. The regular recurring discount begins with subsequent eligible cleanings.
What ongoing recurring discounts are available?
Ongoing Standard Cleaning discounts are 20% for weekly service, 15% for every-other-week service, 12% for every-3-weeks service, and 10% for every-4-weeks service.
How does coupon code SUMMER work?
SUMMER gives eligible new customers $25 off a qualifying cleaning with a minimum $100 service value before discount. No recurring-service commitment is required.
Can I combine RECUR30 and SUMMER?
No. Offers cannot be combined with another coupon or promotion unless Tampa Bay Shine specifically states otherwise.
Can I see my exact cleaning price before booking?
Yes. Online booking calculates your exact service price before you enter payment information. Applicable coupon discounts are reflected according to the selected service and offer eligibility.
When is my card charged?
Your card is typically pre-authorized 1–2 days before the scheduled service and charged after the cleaning is completed.
How does the commercial cleaning offer work?
Qualified new recurring commercial cleaning clients may be eligible for a free first commercial cleaning. Qualification depends on location, cleaning scope, access requirements, recurring-service fit, and account approval. Commercial customers can request a walkthrough through our contact page.
