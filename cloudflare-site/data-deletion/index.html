Data Deletion Instructions

Tampa Bay Shine provides the instructions below for requesting deletion of personal information associated with our website, booking services, and any connected login methods, including Facebook Login if used.

1) Request Data Deletion from Tampa Bay Shine

To request deletion of your personal data, please email us at support@tampabayshine.com with the subject line: Data Deletion Request.

Include the following in your request:

  • Your full name
  • The email address and/or phone number associated with your account or booking
  • Your service address, if applicable, to help us locate the correct booking records
  • If you used Facebook Login: the Facebook profile name or the email address tied to Facebook, if available
  • Any recent booking date(s), if applicable

We will verify your request and delete the personal data associated with your account where applicable. Certain information may be retained when required for legal, tax, accounting, insurance, dispute resolution, or fraud prevention purposes.

2) Remove Tampa Bay Shine Access in Facebook If You Used Facebook Login

If you used Facebook Login and want to remove Tampa Bay Shine’s access from Facebook, you may do so in your Facebook settings:

  1. Open Facebook and go to Settings & privacy
  2. Select Settings
  3. Navigate to Apps and Websites
  4. Find our app, for example Booking Koala, and choose Remove

Removing the app prevents future data sharing from Facebook to Tampa Bay Shine for that login connection. If you also want data deleted from our systems, please submit a request using the email method above.

What Data May Be Deleted

Depending on your relationship with Tampa Bay Shine and applicable requirements, we may delete or anonymize:

  • Account profile information, such as name, email, and phone number
  • Saved booking preferences and contact form submissions
  • Customer portal records where applicable
  • Marketing subscription records, including email and SMS lists

Transactional records related to completed services and payments may be retained as required by law or for legitimate business purposes, such as tax and accounting compliance.
