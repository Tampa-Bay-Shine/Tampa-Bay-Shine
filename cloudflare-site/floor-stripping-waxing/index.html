When Routine Floor Cleaning Is No Longer Enough

Floor stripping and waxing is the reset service for resilient floors that have lost gloss, built up wear patterns, or reached the point where routine maintenance alone will not restore appearance. If your floors still have usable finish and mainly need maintenance, start with Floor Cleaning.

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should VCT be stripped and waxed?

It depends on traffic, maintenance quality, and finish wear. Many facilities need it periodically once routine cleaning and burnishing are no longer enough to restore appearance.

Do you work nights or weekends?

Yes. Most strip-and-wax projects are scheduled after hours or on weekends to reduce operational disruption.

What’s the difference between scrub and recoat versus full strip and wax?

Scrub and recoat is a lighter refresh for floors that still have a workable finish. Strip and wax removes worn finish layers more completely so the floor can be rebuilt properly.

Do you move furniture or fixtures?

Light, reasonable item movement can be handled as scoped. Heavy equipment or specialty items may require coordination ahead of service.

Can this be paired with regular floor maintenance afterward?

Yes. Many clients restore first, then move into a Floor Cleaning maintenance plan to extend the life of the new finish.
