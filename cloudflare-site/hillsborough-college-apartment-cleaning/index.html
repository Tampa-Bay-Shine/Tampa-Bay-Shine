Quick answer

Tampa Bay Shine offers residential apartment cleaning for eligible Hillsborough College students in Tampa, Brandon and SouthShore. Choose standard, deep or move-out cleaning through regular online pricing; code HC20 provides $20 off an eligible first cleaning of at least $125 for a new customer.

Cleaning between classes across Hillsborough County

Hillsborough College serves students through multiple Hillsborough County campuses. Students living in Tampa, Brandon or SouthShore may have different commute patterns and rental arrangements; cleaning availability depends on the individual apartment address, not simply which campus a student attends.

The high-traffic areas roommates share

Kitchens and bathrooms tend to accumulate dishes, grease, soap residue and tracked-in dust. Regular apartment cleaning helps residents keep shared areas comfortable; deeper service may be appropriate after a busy term or an extended period without detailed upkeep.

Move-in and lease-end planning

Before moving into or out of a rental, discuss access, parking and any building instructions. For a move-out visit, remove belongings and trash, review lease requirements and schedule cleaning before the final walkthrough. Cleaning does not guarantee a deposit refund.

Practical support for commuting and working students

Students balancing work, classes and family responsibilities may appreciate a one-time reset rather than a commitment to recurring service. Frequency choices remain available for those who prefer a predictable schedule.

Parents and relatives can help

A cleaning gift card can help a student cover the cost of an eligible cleaning. The $20 student coupon applies to a qualifying cleaning booking, not to buying a gift card.

Planning service across Hillsborough College locations

Hillsborough College students may commute from very different parts of Hillsborough County. A student living in Brandon can have different access and scheduling requirements from someone renting in Tampa or SouthShore. Choose a cleaning visit based on the actual apartment address rather than the campus name. Confirm service-area coverage through the usual booking process, and provide any building access or parking instructions.

If roommates share the rental, agree on the rooms included in the booking and who will arrange authorized entry before the cleaner arrives.

Before booking a student apartment clean

For a maintained occupied apartment, regular cleaning can cover routine shared areas. If the kitchen or bathrooms have more persistent buildup, compare the deep-cleaning scope. For lease-end cleaning, check the property manager's instructions, remove belongings and confirm the unit is ready. Coordinate expenses and the booking contact with roommates in advance.

Frequently asked questions

Do you clean apartments for Hillsborough College students?

Yes, at qualifying residential addresses in the Tampa, Brandon and SouthShore service areas, subject to availability.

Does HC20 apply to any cleaning?

HC20 is for eligible new customers on their first qualifying one-time cleaning or first recurring visit, with a $125 minimum cleaning subtotal and verification as described in the coupon terms.

Can I book for a roommate or my student?

An authorized customer may coordinate booking and access. The student associated with the offer must meet applicable eligibility requirements.

Do you clean classroom buildings or dorms?

This page describes residential cleaning, not college facility contracts. Student housing operated by an institution requires permission for outside service providers.

Can I schedule cleaning before returning apartment keys?

Yes, if an appointment is available; leave time for the visit and any property-manager walkthrough.
