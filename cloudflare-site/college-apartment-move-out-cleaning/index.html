Plan before returning your keys

Check the lease and any cleaning checklist from your property manager. Remove belongings and trash, coordinate keys and confirm the unit will be accessible.

Coordinate with roommates

Decide who will handle booking, payment and access. If someone still occupies the apartment, choose a service matching the actual condition and occupancy.

Allow time for the final walkthrough

Choose a date that leaves time after move-out and before inspection. Move-out cleaning can help prepare a unit but cannot guarantee security-deposit return.
