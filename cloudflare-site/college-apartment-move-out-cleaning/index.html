Quick answer

College students in Tampa can book move-out cleaning for eligible apartments through Tampa Bay Shine. Before cleaning, remove belongings and trash, coordinate roommates and property access, review lease requirements and leave sufficient time before the final walkthrough.

Four steps before your college move-out cleaning

First, read your lease and property-manager instructions. Second, agree with roommates on a move-out date, booking contact and payment. Third, remove possessions and bag up trash. Fourth, arrange authorized entry, parking and any building elevator reservation before the cleaner arrives.

Choose move-out service for the correct property condition

An empty apartment needing turnover preparation is different from a still-occupied apartment that needs routine upkeep. Review our move-out service information and book the service appropriate to the current occupancy and required scope.

Plan for inspections without promising a deposit

A clean apartment can support a smooth final walkthrough, but deductions depend on the lease and property manager. We do not guarantee a security deposit refund, repair damage or replace the property’s official inspection requirements.

Roommate coordination in shared rentals

Assign one primary contact, make sure each room is empty if included, and agree on access. Cleaners cannot move or pack personal belongings as part of a standard cleaning booking.

Parents can help with the cost

A parent or relative can purchase a gift card toward an eligible move-out cleaning. Check the remaining balance and book an appointment in advance of the lease deadline.

Frequently asked questions

How early should I schedule an end-of-semester cleaning?

Book once your move-out date is known; demand and availability can vary around semester changes.

Can you clean before all roommates have left?

Select an appropriate service for the apartment’s actual occupancy; a full move-out cleaning typically assumes spaces are vacant.

Will cleaning guarantee my security deposit?

No. The property manager applies the lease and inspection terms.

Can I book online?

Yes. Use the existing Tampa Bay Shine booking page to see service options and appointment availability.
