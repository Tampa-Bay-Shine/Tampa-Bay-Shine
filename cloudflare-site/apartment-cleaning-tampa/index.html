Tampa Cleaning Questions

Apartment Cleaning FAQs — Tampa, FL

How much does apartment cleaning cost in Tampa?
Standard Cleaning starts at $114 for the lowest one-time configuration. Deep Cleaning starts at $199, and Move-In / Move-Out Cleaning starts at $259. Your exact residential price depends on the property and selected options and is shown online before you enter payment information.
What's included in Standard Apartment Cleaning?
Standard Cleaning covers routine cleaning of kitchens, bathrooms, bedrooms and common living areas, including surface wiping, light dusting, vacuuming, mopping and trash removal. Available extras can be selected during booking.
Do you bring cleaning supplies?
Yes. Tampa Bay Shine brings cleaning supplies for the scheduled service. Customer-provided products or equipment can also be used when requested.
Can I schedule same-day or next-day apartment cleaning?
Appointment availability varies by date, property size and cleaning scope. Check the online booking calendar for current Tampa availability.
Do you coordinate with Tampa high-rise buildings and concierge desks?
Yes. Provide parking, garage, concierge, elevator, loading-zone, gate, leasing-office or other building instructions when booking.
Do you offer move-out apartment cleaning in Tampa?
Yes. Tampa Bay Shine offers detailed move-related cleaning through Apartment Move-Out Cleaning and the dedicated Move-Out Cleaning in Tampa service.
Do you offer recurring apartment cleaning in Tampa?
Yes. Eligible recurring Standard Cleaning can be scheduled weekly, every other week, every 3 weeks or every 4 weeks.
Will I usually have the same cleaner for recurring service?
Tampa Bay Shine generally keeps the same cleaning professional on recurring schedules whenever availability allows. Another background-checked cleaning professional may service the appointment when needed.
When is my card charged?
Your card is typically pre-authorized 1–2 days before the scheduled cleaning and charged after the cleaning is completed.
