Tampa Bay Shine Services FAQs

A Few Things People Ask Before Booking.

Which cleaning should I choose for regular upkeep?

Standard Cleaning is designed for routine one-time or recurring maintenance once your home is at a maintenance-ready baseline.

When should I choose Deep Cleaning?

Deep Cleaning is a better fit for first-time service, seasonal resets, buildup, and homes that need more detail than routine maintenance.

What is the difference between regular Move-Out and Deep Move-In/Move-Out?

Regular Move-Out focuses on a thorough vacant-property handoff. Deep Move-In/Move-Out is the most detailed moving clean for heavier buildup and detailed interior areas.

Can I get residential pricing online?

Yes. Choose Standard, Deep, Move-Out, or Deep Move-In/Move-Out to see pricing and continue booking online.

Do you offer recurring office and commercial cleaning?

Yes. Tampa Bay Shine supports recurring janitorial and commercial cleaning programs, plus one-time and specialty scopes depending on the facility.

Do you clean apartments, condos, and short-term rentals?

Yes. Yes. We clean apartments, condos, and short-term rentals across the Tampa Bay communities we serve.

Which Tampa Bay communities do you serve?

Tampa Bay Shine serves Tampa, South Tampa, Downtown Tampa, Brandon, Riverview, Wesley Chapel, Apollo Beach, Ruskin, Sun City Center, and surrounding Tampa Bay communities.
