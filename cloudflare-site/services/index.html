Cleaning Service Questions

Tampa Bay Shine Services FAQs

Which residential cleaning service should I choose?
Choose Standard Cleaning for routine upkeep, Deep Cleaning for a more detailed reset or heavier buildup, and Move-In / Move-Out Cleaning for vacant properties and final handoffs.
How much does residential cleaning cost?
Standard Cleaning starts at $114, Deep Cleaning starts at $199, and Move-In / Move-Out Cleaning starts at $259. Your exact residential price depends on your property and selected options and is shown during online booking before payment information.
Do you offer recurring house cleaning?
Yes. Standard Cleaning can be scheduled weekly, every other week, every 3 weeks or every 4 weeks. Eligible ongoing frequency discounts range from 10% to 20%.
Do you clean apartments and condos?
Yes. Tampa Bay Shine provides apartment and condo cleaning for routine upkeep, move-outs and other eligible residential cleaning needs.
Do you provide office and commercial cleaning?
Yes. Tampa Bay Shine provides office cleaning, commercial cleaning, common-area cleaning, non-clinical medical-office cleaning and selected specialty commercial services. Commercial clients can request a walkthrough for a custom scope.
Are you insured and are cleaning professionals background-checked?
Tampa Bay Shine is licensed and insured, and the cleaning professionals servicing customer properties are background-checked. Certificates of Insurance are available on request for commercial clients.
Which areas do you serve?
Tampa Bay Shine serves Tampa, South Tampa, Brandon, Riverview, Wesley Chapel, Apollo Beach, Ruskin, Sun City Center, Lutz, Temple Terrace, Bradenton and nearby Tampa Bay communities.
Real estate professionals: Tampa Bay Shine provides Realtor and real estate cleaning services in Tampa for listings, move-outs, move-ins, photo preparation, final walkthroughs and closings.
