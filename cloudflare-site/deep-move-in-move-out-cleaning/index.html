Deep Move-In/Move-Out Cleaning in Tampa Bay

For vacant or mostly vacant homes that need substantially more cleaning than a normally maintained move-out. We complete the full Move-In/Out scope plus added labor for accumulated grease, soap scum, grime, dust and buildup.

Get My Exact Price Compare Cleaning Levels
Quick Answer: Regular Move-In/Out Cleaning is for normally maintained vacant homes. Deep Move-In/Move-Out is the higher-condition tier when accumulated buildup makes the same move-clean scope require substantially more labor.

When to Choose Deep Move-In/Move-Out

Heavy bathroom buildup

Significant soap scum, mineral residue, grime or buildup on showers, tubs, toilets and reachable bathroom surfaces.

Heavily soiled kitchen areas

Accumulated grease, food residue, cabinet-front grime or an unusually dirty oven or refrigerator requiring additional cleaning time.

Neglected surfaces

Accumulated dust and grime on reachable doors, frames, trim, baseboards and other surfaces beyond a normally maintained move-clean condition.

Who This Service Is Designed For

Deep Move-In/Move-Out Cleaning is intended for vacant or mostly vacant houses, apartments and condos where the property condition calls for more labor than a standard move cleaning. It can be useful before a new resident moves in, after a tenant or owner moves out, or when a property needs a more intensive reset before listing, closing, inspection or turnover.

Examples include a kitchen with layers of cooking residue, bathrooms with substantial soap scum or mineral buildup, neglected baseboards and trim, or appliances that need considerably more detailing than would be expected in a reasonably maintained home. The service level is based on the current condition of the property rather than the home's age or the length of the move itself.

If the home is occupied and needs a detailed reset rather than a vacancy-focused move clean, review our Deep Cleaning service. If the property is vacant and reasonably maintained, our Tampa Bay Move-In/Move-Out Cleaning overview explains the standard move-clean option.

What the Higher-Condition Tier Covers

Deep Move-In/Move-Out is not a second cleaning of the same areas. The additional service level accounts for the labor required to remove heavier accumulated buildup before completing the full move-in/out scope.

  • Full confirmed Move-In/Out cleaning scope
  • Additional labor for heavier grease and food residue
  • Additional labor for soap scum, mineral residue and bathroom grime
  • Additional labor for accumulated dust, grime and neglected reachable surfaces
  • Detailed appliance, cabinet, baseboard, closet, bathroom and final-floor work within the confirmed scope

Preparing the Property for a Deep Move Cleaning

The service works best when the property is vacant or mostly vacant and cleaners can reach the areas included in the confirmed scope. Personal belongings, moving boxes, loose trash and items that will remain in cabinets or closets can limit access and reduce the amount of detailed cleaning that can be completed efficiently.

Before the appointment, make sure utilities needed for cleaning are available and provide any required entry instructions, gate information, lockbox details, parking directions or building-access procedures. If there are unusual conditions, extensive buildup, excessive debris, or areas you are unsure about, sharing that information before service helps determine whether the deep move-clean tier is appropriate.

For a room-by-room comparison of our service levels, see the Tampa Bay Shine cleaning checklist. You can also review our move-out cleaning cost and time guide for factors that can affect the scope and labor required for a move cleaning.

Conditions That Require Assessment

This service does not include mold remediation, biohazard cleanup, pest infestation remediation, hoarding cleanup, excessive trash or debris removal, or specialized restoration work. Extremely neglected properties or unusual conditions may require photos and a custom assessment before service.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Deep Move-In/Move-Out Cleaning?

It is the full Move-In/Out scope plus additional labor for significant accumulated grease, soap scum, grime, dust and buildup in a vacant or mostly vacant home.

When should I choose it instead of regular Move-In/Move-Out?

Choose the deep tier when current property condition will require materially more labor than a normally maintained move-clean. Roughly six months without a thorough deep cleaning can be a useful signal, but the home's actual condition is the deciding factor.

Does it include mold remediation?

No. Mold remediation and other specialized remediation work are outside the cleaning scope.

Does the property need to be empty?

The service is intended for vacant or mostly vacant properties so the move-clean areas can be accessed efficiently.

Choose the Right Move Cleaning for Your Home

Significant buildup? Choose Deep Move-In/Move-Out Cleaning when booking. If the vacant home has been reasonably maintained, choose regular Move-In/Out Cleaning.
