Before Cleaning Day

How to Prepare Your Apartment for Move-Out Cleaning

1

Empty Cabinets & Drawers

Cabinet and drawer interiors must be empty for interior wipe-down and detailing.

2

Remove Remaining Belongings

Remove personal items, leftover food and loose trash so floors, closets and detailed surfaces can be reached.

3

Keep Utilities Available

Leave water and electricity on when possible so bathrooms, appliances and floors can be properly serviced.

4

Share Building Instructions

Provide gate codes, elevator windows, loading-zone rules, parking instructions and leasing-office requirements.

Apartment Move-Out Questions

Apartment Move-Out & Move-In Cleaning FAQs

How much does apartment move-out cleaning cost?
Move-In / Move-Out Cleaning starts at $259. Your exact residential price depends on the property and selected options and is shown online before you enter payment information.
Do you follow a leasing-office move-out checklist?
Yes. Share the leasing-office or property-manager checklist before service so priorities can be matched to the confirmed cleaning scope. Common inspection areas include empty cabinet and drawer interiors, appliances, bathrooms, baseboards, doors, switches, closets and floors.
Do cabinets and drawers need to be empty?
Yes. Cabinet and drawer interiors must be empty for interior cleaning. If they are not empty, access to those interior surfaces will be limited.
Can you provide an invoice for my landlord or property manager?
Yes. Tampa Bay Shine can provide an invoice showing the service date and scope completed for lease files or proof of professional cleaning.
What if my building has elevator rules, gate codes or loading-zone restrictions?
Provide elevator windows, gate codes, parking rules, loading-zone instructions, concierge procedures and other building requirements when booking.
Should the apartment be empty before cleaning?
Move-Out Cleaning is designed for an empty or substantially empty apartment or condo. Removing belongings gives better access to cabinets, closets, baseboards, floors and appliances.
Do you also provide apartment move-in cleaning?
Yes. Move-In Cleaning can detail the kitchen, bathrooms, empty cabinets, closets, fixtures and floors before you unpack.
Do you bring cleaning supplies?
Yes. Tampa Bay Shine brings cleaning supplies for the scheduled service. Customer-provided products or equipment can also be used when requested.
Do you offer recurring apartment cleaning after move-in?
Yes. Eligible recurring Standard Cleaning can be scheduled weekly, every other week, every 3 weeks or every 4 weeks.
What if an included area is missed?
Report a qualifying missed area from the original confirmed checklist within 24 hours. Tampa Bay Shine can return to re-clean that specific qualifying area at no additional charge. Permanent stains, damage, conditions outside the cleaner's control and areas affected after service are excluded.
