Property Management Cleaning FAQs

What cleaning services do you provide for property managers?
Property-management cleaning can combine recurring common-area cleaning, vacant-unit move-in or move-out cleaning, leasing and management office cleaning, amenity cleaning, floor care and window cleaning. The exact scope is built around the property and management requirements.
Do you clean apartment and multifamily common areas?
Yes. Common-area programs can cover lobbies, hallways, elevators, stairwells, mail and package rooms, clubhouses, fitness rooms, shared restrooms and other resident-facing spaces.
Can you handle vacant apartment turnovers?
Yes. Vacant-unit cleaning can be coordinated for move-outs, move-ins and make-ready schedules. Scope and timing depend on unit condition, access and the requested cleaning level.
Can you work with multiple properties?
Yes. Tampa Bay Shine can discuss recurring scopes for individual buildings or multiple managed properties within our service area, with access and service requirements documented by location.
Can you provide a certificate of insurance?
Yes. Certificates of Insurance are available on request and can name the property owner, association or management company as needed.
Do you offer recurring janitorial schedules?
Yes. Recurring schedules are customized around property traffic, amenity use, management expectations and access requirements.
Do you clean leasing or property-management offices?
Yes. Management and leasing offices can be included in a property scope or handled through our office cleaning service.
What Tampa Bay areas do you serve for property-management cleaning?
Service is available across established Tampa Bay Shine service areas including Tampa, Downtown Tampa, South Tampa, Brandon, Riverview, Apollo Beach, Wesley Chapel, Ruskin, Sun City Center, Lutz, Temple Terrace and Bradenton, subject to route and scheduling availability.
