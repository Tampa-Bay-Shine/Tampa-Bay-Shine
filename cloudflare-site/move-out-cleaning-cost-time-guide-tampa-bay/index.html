Planning Ranges

How Long Does Move-Out Cleaning Take?

Most move-out cleanings take approximately 2–8+ hours depending on property size, condition, access and the amount of detailed interior work. Empty properties with utilities on and access confirmed in advance are generally easier to service.

Property Size Typical Planning Range What Can Add Time?
Studio / 1 Bedroom 2–4 hours Kitchen grease, soap scum, appliance interiors, pet hair or high-rise access timing.
2 Bedrooms / 2 Bathrooms 3–5 hours Multiple bathrooms, cabinet interiors, blinds, baseboards, hard-water buildup or balcony detail.
3 Bedrooms / 2 Bathrooms 4–6 hours Larger kitchens, extra detailing, doors, baseboards, interior windows or garage work.
4+ Bedrooms / Larger Homes 5–8+ hours Higher square footage, multiple bathrooms, bonus rooms, heavy buildup or tight walkthrough deadlines.
These are planning ranges, not appointment guarantees. Exact cleaning time depends on the selected scope, property condition, access and crew configuration.
Pricing Variables

What Affects Move-Out Cleaning Cost?

Move-out cleaning pricing and service time can change significantly based on the property, its condition, the selected detail work and access requirements.

1

Size & Layout

More bedrooms, bathrooms, square footage, stairs and bonus areas generally require more labor time.

2

Current Condition

Kitchen buildup, soap scum, pet hair, hard-water scale and heavier dust can increase detailed cleaning time.

3

Interior Detail

Appliance interiors, cabinets, drawers, closets and shelving can add substantial detail compared with routine cleaning.

4

Selected Add-Ons

Interior windows, tracks, blinds, ceiling fans, vents, patios, balconies or garage sweep-outs can affect the overall scope.

5

Access Logistics

Elevators, loading areas, gated communities, parking rules and lockbox procedures can affect scheduling and usable cleaning time.

6

Deadlines

Same-day requests, closing appointments, key-return deadlines and final walkthrough windows can require additional coordination.

Scope Matters

What Should You Confirm Before Booking?

Move-out cleaning is more detailed than routine maintenance because it prepares an empty or substantially empty property for a lease handoff, listing, closing or move-in.

Common Move-Out Priorities

  • Kitchen surfaces, sink, cabinet fronts and appliance exteriors
  • Appliance interiors and empty cabinet or drawer interiors when included in the confirmed scope
  • Bathrooms, tubs, showers, toilets, vanities, mirrors and fixtures
  • Baseboards, doors, switches, handles, closet shelving and final floors

Details to Confirm

  • Interior windows, slider tracks, blinds, fans and vents
  • Balcony, patio, garage or exterior-entry work
  • Heavy buildup, paint marks, adhesive residue or leftover debris
  • Elevator reservations, parking, gates, loading procedures and lockbox access

Better Pricing Information

How to Get the Most Accurate Move-Out Cleaning Price

Property Details to Provide

  • Square footage
  • Bedroom and bathroom count
  • Whether the property will be empty, substantially empty or still furnished
  • Photos of kitchens, bathrooms, floors or problem areas when requesting a custom quote
  • Walkthrough, closing or key-return deadline

Prepare the Property

  • Remove personal belongings from cabinets, drawers, closets and appliances
  • Keep water and electricity on
  • Send parking, gate, elevator, concierge, lockbox or property-manager instructions
  • Share landlord, leasing-office or inspection priorities before the appointment

Cost & Time Questions

Move-Out Cleaning Cost & Time FAQs

How long does move-out cleaning usually take?
Many move-out cleanings fall within a planning range of about 2–8+ hours. Property size, condition, bathrooms, detailed interior work, selected options, access requirements and crew configuration can all affect the actual time.
What affects the cost of move-out cleaning?
Important factors include property size and layout, current condition, detailed interior tasks, selected add-ons, access logistics and the required completion deadline.
Are the refrigerator, oven and cabinet interiors always included?
Appliance interiors and empty cabinet or drawer interiors are common move-out priorities, but confirm the exact cleaning scope and selected options when booking.
How can I get an accurate move-out cleaning price quickly?
Use online booking for eligible residential pricing. For larger properties, heavy buildup, unusual access requirements or tight deadlines, provide the property size, condition, photos and access details when requesting a custom quote.
What should I do before the cleaners arrive?
Remove remaining belongings, empty areas that require interior cleaning, keep water and power on and provide gate, parking, elevator, lockbox or property-manager instructions before service.
Why can two homes of the same size take different amounts of time?
Condition, number of bathrooms, appliance buildup, pet hair, cabinet interiors, blinds, floor detail and access logistics can change labor time even when two properties have similar square footage.
Is move-out cleaning different from Deep Cleaning?
Yes. Deep Cleaning is generally intended for an occupied home that needs additional detail. Move-In / Move-Out Cleaning is designed for an empty or substantially empty property and focuses on vacancy-related details and the final handoff.

Does the Property Need Deep Move-In/Move-Out Cleaning?

Regular Move-In/Out Cleaning is intended for a normally maintained vacant or mostly vacant property. If there is significant accumulated grease, soap scum, grime, dust, heavily soiled appliances or bathrooms, choose Deep Move-In/Move-Out Cleaning so the additional labor is included in the service level.
