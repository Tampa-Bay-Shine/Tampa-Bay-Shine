CLEAR COMMERCIAL SCOPE

What goes into a member-ready facility?

A quote defines exactly which zones, surfaces and tasks are covered. We focus on practical day-to-day upkeep, rather than promising specialized sanitization results or unapproved equipment treatment.

Need dedicated restroom attendants or restroom-only service? Explore commercial restroom cleaning.

Typical scope considerations

  • Member check-in desks, door hardware and common touchpoints
  • Accessible cardio and strength equipment contact surfaces
  • Mirrors, interior glass and visible smudges
  • Vacuuming, dust removal and compatible floor cleaning
  • Locker-room fixtures, restroom cleaning and trash removal
  • Client-supplied soap, liner and paper restocking where requested
  • Higher-detail tasks or special floor care quoted separately

COMMON QUESTIONS

Gym cleaning FAQs

Do you clean gyms before opening or after closing?

Early-morning and after-hours cleaning may be available based on staffing, site access and the agreed service schedule. Tell us when your facility needs coverage.

Can you clean exercise equipment and shared touchpoints?

Yes. Accessible touchpoints and equipment exterior surfaces can be included using methods compatible with manufacturer guidance and the confirmed scope. Specialized equipment maintenance is not included.

Are locker rooms, showers and restrooms included?

They can be. We can build a scope that covers locker-room surfaces, showers, toilets, sinks, mirrors, floors and trash, with client-provided supply restocking where agreed.

Can you clean rubber gym flooring and studio floors?

Routine floor cleaning can be tailored to rubber, vinyl and other flooring types after reviewing the materials and manufacturer care instructions. Restoration or specialty treatments require separate evaluation.

Do you offer recurring fitness center janitorial service?

Yes. We can discuss recurring cleaning programs for gyms, boutique studios, community fitness rooms and fitness centers, with a custom schedule and scope.

How is a gym cleaning quote calculated?

Pricing depends on the facility size, spaces and surfaces included, traffic, cleaning frequency, access windows and special requirements. Contact us with your facility details for a tailored proposal.
