Core Service Scope

Move-in and move-out cleaning designed for sale preparation, tenant turns, listing presentation, and handoffs.

Common Inclusions

  • Kitchen: counters, sink, exterior cabinets, and exterior appliances
  • Bathrooms: disinfect, fixtures, mirrors, toilets, tubs, and showers
  • Floors: vacuum and mop where applicable
  • Dusting: reachable surfaces, doors, switches, and handles
  • Trash: bag to onsite bin where available

Popular Add-Ons

  • Inside fridge and oven
  • Inside cabinets and drawers
  • Interior windows within safe reach
  • Wall spot cleaning as scoped
  • Heavy baseboard detailing

How It Works

  1. Request or book online and share photos, notes, access details, and timing constraints.
  2. We align the scope for move-in, move-out, listing prep, photo prep, or turnover cleaning.
  3. Access is coordinated through lockbox, smart lock, code, onsite contact, or agent instructions.
  4. Checklist execution focuses on handoff readiness and high-visibility areas.
  5. Completion confirmation is available, with before/after photos upon request.
