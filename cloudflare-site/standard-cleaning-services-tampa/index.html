Cleaning Scope

What’s Included in Standard Cleaning

Standard Cleaning covers routine cleaning throughout kitchens, bathrooms, bedrooms and common living areas. For the full service comparison, use our Cleaning Checklist.

Kitchen

  • Countertops and backsplash wiped
  • Appliance exteriors cleaned
  • Inside microwave cleaned
  • Sinks and fixtures cleaned
  • Floors vacuumed and mopped
  • Trash removed and trash-can exterior wiped

Inside oven, inside refrigerator and inside cabinets are available as selected add-ons.

Bathrooms

  • Vanities, sinks and mirrors cleaned
  • Showers, tubs and toilets cleaned
  • Chrome and glass wiped
  • Floors vacuumed and mopped
  • Trash removed

Bedrooms

  • Light dusting of reachable surfaces
  • Beds made when linens are available
  • Mirrors and shelves wiped
  • Carpets vacuumed and hard floors mopped
  • Trash removed

Living & Common Areas

  • Light dusting of furniture and decor
  • Glass and touchpoint surfaces wiped
  • Floors vacuumed and mopped
  • General tidy of common living areas

Popular Add-Ons

  • Inside oven
  • Inside refrigerator
  • Interior windows
  • Inside cabinets when empty
  • Additional detail work when available

Common Questions

Standard House Cleaning FAQs

What’s included in Standard Cleaning?
Standard Cleaning covers routine cleaning in kitchens, bathrooms, bedrooms and living areas, including surface wiping, light dusting, vacuuming, mopping and trash removal. Optional add-ons are available for services such as inside oven, inside refrigerator, interior windows and inside empty cabinets.
How much does Standard Cleaning cost?
Standard Cleaning starts at $114 for the lowest one-time configuration: an under-1,000-square-foot, 1-bedroom, 1-bathroom home. Your exact price is calculated online based on your home and selected options before you enter payment information.
How does the RELAX30 recurring-cleaning offer work?
RELAX30 gives eligible new customers 30% off the first recurring cleaning with a minimum $100 service value before discount. The customer must complete 3 total cleanings: the discounted first cleaning plus 2 additional recurring cleanings. RELAX30 does not stack with the normal frequency discount on the first visit. If the required 3 cleanings are not completed, the original 30% introductory discount may be charged back.
What recurring cleaning discounts do you offer?
Ongoing Standard Cleaning discounts are 20% for weekly service, 15% for bi-weekly (every-other-week) service, 12% for every-3-weeks service, and 10% for every-4-weeks service. When RELAX30 is used, the normal frequency discount begins after the introductory first cleaning.
Can I get a discount without signing up for recurring service?
New customers who prefer one-time service can use code SUMMER for $25 off a qualifying cleaning with a minimum $100 service value before discount. SUMMER does not require a recurring commitment.
Do you bring cleaning supplies and equipment?
Yes. Tampa Bay Shine brings standard cleaning supplies and equipment. We can also use customer-provided supplies or equipment when requested.
Will I usually have the same cleaner for recurring service?
We generally keep the same cleaning professional on your recurring schedule whenever availability allows. If that professional is unavailable and the appointment cannot be moved, another background-checked cleaning professional may complete the service.
When is my card charged?
Your card is typically pre-authorized 1–2 days before the scheduled cleaning and charged after the cleaning is completed.
What does the 24-hour satisfaction guarantee cover?
If an area included in the original service checklist was missed or not cleaned to expectations, report it within 24 hours of service completion and Tampa Bay Shine will return to re-clean that specific area at no additional cost. Limitations apply to permanent stains, damage, residue, conditions outside our control, and situations where third parties entered the property after our visit.
What is the difference between Standard Cleaning and Deep Cleaning?
Standard Cleaning is designed for routine upkeep. Deep Cleaning adds more detailed work for buildup and areas such as baseboards, blinds, fans, edges and heavier kitchen or bathroom cleaning.
Where do you provide Standard Cleaning?
Tampa Bay Shine provides Standard and recurring house cleaning in Tampa, South Tampa, Downtown Tampa, Brandon, Riverview, Wesley Chapel, Apollo Beach, Temple Terrace, Ruskin, Sun City Center, Lutz, Temple Terrace, Bradenton and nearby Tampa Bay communities, subject to scheduling and service availability.
