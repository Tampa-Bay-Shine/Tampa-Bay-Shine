Apartment Cleaning FAQs — Apollo Beach, FL

How much does apartment cleaning cost in Apollo Beach?
Standard Cleaning starts at $114 for the lowest one-time configuration. Deep Cleaning starts at $199, and Move-In / Move-Out Cleaning starts at $259. Your exact residential price depends on the property and selected options and is shown online before you enter payment information.
How do you handle sand and entry grit near the water?
Interior entry areas can be vacuumed and floors can be mopped as part of the selected cleaning scope. Slider tracks, balcony areas and related exterior-facing details can be addressed when those options are included.
Can I schedule same-day or next-day apartment cleaning?
Appointment availability varies by date, property size and cleaning scope. Check the online booking calendar for current Apollo Beach availability.
Can you work with my HOA or COA for access and parking?
Yes. Provide gate instructions, visitor-pass requirements, parking rules, elevator reservations and other community access requirements when booking.
What is different about move-out apartment cleaning?
Move-out apartment cleaning is intended for an empty or substantially empty property and includes more detail than routine maintenance cleaning. Tampa Bay Shine also offers dedicated Move-Out Cleaning in Apollo Beach.
Do you offer recurring apartment cleaning in Apollo Beach?
Yes. Eligible recurring Standard Cleaning can be scheduled weekly, every other week, every 3 weeks or every 4 weeks.
