How to Choose a Cleaning Company in Tampa: 7 Things to Check Before You Hire

Compare providers by scope, insurance, communication and clear pricing.

Compare providers by scope, insurance, communication and clear pricing.

Seven things to check

Ask for a written checklist; verify insurance and coverage; understand who performs the cleaning; compare what is included in the price; review cancellation terms; ask how missed areas are handled; and confirm booking and communication options.

Compare the actual scope

A low starting price may exclude appliance interiors, cabinet interiors, extra bathrooms and heavily soiled areas. Request a written scope so you can compare like for like.

Match the service to the home

A maintained home may need standard cleaning. Accumulated buildup may warrant deep cleaning. Vacant properties may require move-related cleaning.

Before the first appointment

Share square footage, bathrooms, pets, access details, priority areas and unusual buildup. Clarify whether the cleaner supplies products and equipment.

Questions after receiving the quote

Is the price confirmed before booking? Which tasks are excluded? How do schedule changes work? What is the reporting window for a missed area? Keep the answers in writing.
