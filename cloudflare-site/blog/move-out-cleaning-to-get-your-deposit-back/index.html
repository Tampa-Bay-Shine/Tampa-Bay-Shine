Move-Out Cleaning and Your Security Deposit: A Tampa Bay Renter's Guide

A complete cleaning, documentation and final-walkthrough plan for renters preparing to hand back a Tampa Bay apartment, condo or rental home.

Tampa Bay Shine field guidePractical guidance based on the cleaning services we provide across Tampa Bay.
Quick answer

A strong move-out clean should leave an empty rental free of ordinary dirt, grease, dust, hair, residue and trash, with special attention to kitchens, bathrooms, appliance interiors, cabinets, baseboards and floors. Cleaning alone cannot guarantee a security-deposit refund: landlords may also consider damage, unpaid charges, missing items, lease requirements and other conditions. Follow your written move-out instructions, clean after belongings are removed, document the finished condition and leave time to correct anything discovered before you surrender the keys.

Before cleaning, find out what your property actually requires

Do not start with a generic internet checklist if your landlord or property manager has given you written instructions. Review your lease, resident portal, move-out email and any inspection checklist first. Those documents may identify property-specific tasks such as cleaning appliance interiors, emptying cabinets, removing adhesive, returning parking credentials or scheduling an elevator.

Separate the job into three categories: cleaning, repairs or damage, and administrative move-out tasks. A cleaner can remove ordinary soil and buildup, but cleaning does not patch holes, replace broken blinds, repair damaged flooring or resolve unpaid rent.

Best sequence

Move belongings out first, remove trash, complete repairs that could create dust, then perform the detailed cleaning. Do final floor work near the end so movers and repair work do not immediately soil freshly cleaned surfaces.

What tends to matter during a final walkthrough?

Every property is different, but an empty rental makes details visible that were easy to miss while furnished. Grease around a range, crumbs inside drawers, soap residue in a shower, dust on baseboards, hair around bathroom fixtures and debris in closets become much more obvious.

Think from the inspector's point of view. Empty cabinets can be opened, appliance interiors are visible, and the full floor perimeter is exposed. That is why a move-out clean differs from routine maintenance cleaning.

Room-by-room move-out cleaning checklist

Kitchen

  • Remove food, liners and personal items from cabinets and drawers.
  • Wipe accessible cabinet and drawer interiors and exterior fronts.
  • Degrease counters, backsplash and accessible exterior surfaces.
  • Clean sink and faucet, including residue around edges and fixtures.
  • Clean cooktop/range exterior and oven interior when required by scope.
  • Empty and clean refrigerator interior when required.
  • Check floor edges, toe kicks and areas where movable items were located.

Bathrooms

  • Remove toiletries and trash.
  • Address soap film and ordinary buildup on showers, tubs and surrounds.
  • Clean toilets, sinks, counters, mirrors and accessible fixtures.
  • Wipe empty vanity storage when required.
  • Check baseboards, floor edges and hair around corners and fixtures.

Bedrooms, living areas and closets

  • Dust accessible horizontal surfaces, ledges and baseboards.
  • Wipe empty closet shelves where included.
  • Remove cobwebs and visible dust.
  • Check doors, handles and switch areas for ordinary soil.
  • Vacuum or mop the full accessible floor after furniture is gone.

Final details

  • Remove every bag of trash and all personal property.
  • Check balconies, patios, garages or storage areas if required by your lease.
  • Look inside every cabinet, closet, drawer and appliance before leaving.
  • Walk the property in good lighting and take dated photos or video.

Cleaning versus damage

Cleaning addresses removable soil: dust, grease, residue, hair, crumbs and ordinary buildup. It does not restore damaged materials.

Document the condition

After the home is empty and cleaning is complete, photograph each room from more than one angle. Capture the kitchen, appliance interiors if relevant, bathrooms, closets and floors. Keep your move-out instructions and cleaning receipt. Documentation does not decide a deposit dispute by itself, but it gives you a clearer record of the condition in which you returned the property.

If the property offers a pre-move-out inspection, use it. It can reveal property-specific expectations while you still have time to address them.

When should you schedule move-out cleaning?

The ideal window is usually after movers remove furniture and boxes but before your keys are due. An empty home gives cleaners access to floor edges, closets, cabinets and areas blocked during occupied cleaning.

Avoid scheduling the cleaner at the exact moment movers, painters or repair vendors are working throughout the property. Preserve a buffer after cleaning for your own inspection and any last property-specific task.

Tampa Bay apartment and condo logistics

High-rise and multifamily properties can add logistics that single-family homes do not have. Confirm vendor parking, gate access, concierge procedures, elevator reservations and loading rules before the appointment. In Downtown Tampa and other managed buildings, access delays can consume cleaning time.

Keep electricity and water active through cleaning and inspection. Cleaners need lighting, running water and power for normal work.

DIY or professional move-out cleaning?

DIY can make sense when the property is small, already maintained and you have enough time after moving. Professional cleaning becomes more useful when you are balancing movers, work, travel, a larger property, accumulated buildup or a tight key-return deadline.

Hiring a cleaner does not transfer responsibility for the lease to the cleaning company. Review the scope, communicate special requirements and inspect afterward.

Common move-out cleaning mistakes

  • Cleaning too early: movers can track dirt back through the home.
  • Leaving items behind: interiors cannot be fully cleaned while still full.
  • Ignoring the written checklist: property-specific instructions should control.
  • Forgetting appliance interiors: confirm whether they are expected and included.
  • Using the wrong product: aggressive chemicals can damage stone, flooring or finishes.
  • Skipping your own final walkthrough: inspect before surrendering access when possible.

Move-out cleaning FAQ

Does professional cleaning guarantee my security deposit?

No. Cleaning addresses ordinary dirt and buildup, but deposit decisions can also involve damage, lease terms, missing property and unpaid charges.

Should the apartment be completely empty?

Empty or substantially empty is best because it gives access to closets, cabinets, floor edges and previously blocked areas.

Before or after movers?

Detailed cleaning is generally most effective after movers are finished. Save final floors for near the end.

What if the leasing office has its own checklist?

Use it. Property-specific written requirements should take priority over a generic checklist, and unusual tasks should be confirmed with the cleaner before the appointment.
