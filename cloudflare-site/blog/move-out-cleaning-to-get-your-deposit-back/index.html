Move-Out Cleaning and Your Security Deposit

A practical guide to cleaning, documentation and final-walkthrough preparation for Tampa Bay renters.

Quick answer

Professional move-out cleaning can help you return a rental in cleaner condition, but no cleaning company can guarantee a security-deposit refund. Deposit decisions can also involve damage, lease terms, unpaid charges and other factors outside cleaning.

Start with your lease and move-out instructions

Review the lease, resident portal and any written checklist from the landlord or property manager. Those instructions should control your final preparation. Pay attention to appliance interiors, cabinets, bathrooms, floors, trash removal, keys and the condition expected at the walkthrough.

What a move-out clean should focus on

For an empty or substantially empty property, high-value work includes kitchen surfaces, appliance interiors when included, empty cabinet and drawer interiors, bathroom buildup, baseboards, closet shelving and final floors.

Cleaning versus damage

Cleaning addresses soil, dust, grease, residue and ordinary buildup. It does not repair holes, broken fixtures, permanent stains or damaged flooring. Photograph the property after belongings are removed and again after cleaning.

A practical final-walkthrough sequence

  1. Remove belongings and trash before detailed cleaning.
  2. Keep electricity and water on through cleaning and inspection.
  3. Empty appliances, cabinets, drawers and closets that need interior cleaning.
  4. Provide parking, gate, elevator or lockbox instructions.
  5. Walk the property in good light and photograph each room.
  6. Complete landlord-specific tasks outside the cleaning scope.

When to book

Schedule after movers are finished whenever possible, while leaving time before key return to address anything found during the final walkthrough.
