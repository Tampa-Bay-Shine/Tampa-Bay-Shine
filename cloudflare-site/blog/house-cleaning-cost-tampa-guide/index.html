What affects the price

Square footage, bedrooms and bathrooms, home condition, cleaning type, visit frequency, extras and property access all influence the amount of work.

Why starting prices vary

An advertised starting price may assume a small, maintained home with limited extras. Compare the included tasks rather than relying on a headline price.

Standard versus deep versus move-related cleaning

Standard cleaning maintains an already-kept home. Deep cleaning addresses accumulated detail work. Move-in/out cleaning focuses on vacant-property needs.

Recurring frequency and budget

Weekly or biweekly visits may reduce buildup between appointments. Review the current recurring terms and total cost over several visits.

Get a quote based on your actual home

Enter the correct size, bathrooms, service type, frequency and extras. Flag unusual conditions before booking. Review the final scope and price at online booking.

Beware of misleading averages

Published local averages often combine different service types, property sizes and business models. Your specific scope matters more than a generalized market range.
