A guest-ready turnover combines cleaning, linens, restocking and a final inspection.

Plan the checkout-to-check-in window

Record guest departure and arrival times, access instructions, unit size, parking and laundry requirements. Leave a buffer for late checkouts or unexpected messes.

Room-by-room turnover checklist

Change bed linens and towels; clean bathrooms, kitchens and floors; remove trash; inspect under beds; wipe high-touch surfaces; and reset furniture and guest amenities.

Linen logistics

Keep enough spare linen sets for back-to-back reservations. Confirm whether laundry is completed on site, off site or through a swap system.

Restocking and damage reporting

Use a written supply inventory for toiletries, paper products and trash bags. Agree on how missing items, damage and maintenance concerns are documented.

Final guest-readiness inspection

Check bathrooms and kitchens for hair or residue, confirm clean linens, remove trash, verify access devices and inspect the entry area before guest arrival.

Clarify what is not included

Repairs, pest treatment, major stain removal, guest messaging and supply purchasing are separate unless included in the service agreement. See Airbnb cleaning services.
