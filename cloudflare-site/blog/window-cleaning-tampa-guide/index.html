1. What should professional window cleaning include?

Start by separating the surfaces you want cleaned. A standard glass-only visit and a detail visit involving screens and tracks are not necessarily the same job. At Tampa Bay Shine, service can be arranged for interior-only, exterior-only or combined window cleaning, depending on the property and confirmed scope.

Interior glass

Glass facing into the home or condo, including fingerprints, dust and ordinary surface buildup. Let the cleaner know about furniture, blinds, delicate window coverings and unusual access.

Exterior glass

Accessible outside panes affected by rain, pollen, dirt and irrigation spray. Height, landscaping, screens and obstructions matter when confirming what can be safely reached.

Screens, tracks & sills

Screens can collect dust and debris. Tracks and sills may need separate detailing, especially after extended buildup. Confirm whether these are included or quoted as extras.

Before you book: Ask for a clear breakdown of interior glass, exterior glass, screen handling, track detail, sill wiping, access limitations and any exclusions. That makes comparing estimates more meaningful than comparing prices alone.

2. What affects window-cleaning pricing in Tampa?

There is no responsible flat price for every property. The cost depends on actual window count and configuration, number of panes, accessibility, buildup, requested detailing and whether the service is one-time or recurring.

  • Window count and layout: French panes, sliders, large picture windows and glass doors can require different levels of work.
  • Interior, exterior or both: Cleaning both sides involves a different scope from exterior-only service.
  • Height and access: Stairs, screened enclosures, landscaping, obstacles or upper-story windows can affect safe access and scheduling.
  • Detail level: Screens, tracks, sills and frames should be specifically discussed.
  • Frequency: Ongoing maintenance can reduce buildup between visits.

3. Why do Tampa windows get dirty so quickly?

Our climate creates several sources of recurring residue: seasonal pollen, rain that moves dust across the glass, humidity, nearby landscaping and sprinkler overspray. Coastal exposure may add salt-related deposits in some locations.

Not every mark is removable with ordinary window cleaning. Hard-water mineral etching, scratches, failed insulated glass seals and pre-existing glass damage can remain after surface cleaning. Point out these issues during quoting so the service expectations stay realistic.

4. How to prepare your home before the cleaner arrives

  1. Make a window list. Identify rooms and whether you want inside, outside or both.
  2. Clear access. Move fragile décor from sills and remove small obstacles near windows where practical.
  3. Secure pets. Let the cleaner know which areas need special attention.
  4. Disclose concerns. Mention cracked panes, damaged screens, security bars, very high windows, difficult landscaping or irrigation spots.
  5. Confirm the details. Ask about screen removal, track buildup, parking, gated access and water availability where relevant.

5. When is the best time to schedule?

Window cleaning is particularly useful before hosting, home listing photography, move-in preparation, seasonal cleaning and property inspections. Recurring service can be helpful when trees, traffic, construction dust or irrigation make buildup noticeable. The right schedule depends on your exposure and preferences, not a universal rule.

Common window-cleaning questions

Can I book exterior-only or interior-only window cleaning?

Yes. Both are possible depending on access and the scope confirmed for your property.

Do cleaners automatically include screens and tracks?

Do not assume so. Request those items explicitly so they can be included in the agreed scope and quote.

Can every stain or cloudy pane be restored?

No. Etched glass, scratches, damaged seals and some mineral deposits may not be corrected by standard window cleaning.

Do you clean commercial windows?

Yes. Tampa Bay Shine also handles commercial glass cleaning up to 20 feet where safely accessible. Larger or special-access jobs need individual review.
