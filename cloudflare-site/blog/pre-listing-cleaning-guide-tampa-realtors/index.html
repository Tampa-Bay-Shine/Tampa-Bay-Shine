Pre-Listing Cleaning in Tampa: A Practical Guide for Realtors and Sellers

Plan cleaning around repairs, staging, photography, showings and handoff.

Best timing before listing photos

Complete repairs, painting and dust-producing work first. Declutter and stage the property, then clean and inspect before the photographer arrives.

High-impact cleaning priorities

Prioritize kitchen counters and cabinet fronts, bathroom fixtures and mirrors, floors, accessible baseboards, visible glass, entry areas and frequently touched surfaces.

Occupied versus vacant properties

An occupied listing often benefits from a detailed initial clean and light touch-ups before showings. An empty home provides access to floor edges, cabinets and appliances, but these tasks should be agreed in advance.

Cleaning versus repairs

Ordinary cleaning cannot correct damaged grout, permanent staining, mold problems, damaged finishes or painting needs. Keep repair work separate from the cleaning scope.

Coordinate the closing handoff

Confirm who will schedule the final clean, whether movers will have finished, and what access arrangements are needed. See our Realtor resources.
