Quick answer

Tampa Bay Shine provides cleaning for eligible off-campus student apartments in Tampa, Temple Terrace, Downtown Tampa and South Tampa. Students can book standard, deep or move-out cleaning online; families can purchase cleaning gift cards. On-campus service is available only when residence policies permit outside providers.

Cleaning for student apartments near Tampa colleges

Students living near the University of South Florida, University of Tampa and other Tampa-area campuses often share kitchens, bathrooms and living spaces. Routine professional cleaning can make shared spaces easier to maintain between classes, jobs and roommate schedules. We serve qualifying residential addresses; service-area availability is confirmed through booking.

Which cleaning works best for a student rental?

Standard cleaning is for ongoing upkeep in occupied apartments. A deep cleaning addresses accumulated buildup in high-use areas; move-in and move-out services are intended for changes in occupancy. Review what each service includes and select the option matching the apartment’s condition rather than relying on the college name.

Roommates, building access and on-campus policies

Before an appointment, agree which roommate will book, how cleaners will enter the apartment and whether parking or elevator instructions are needed. Campus residence halls and university-operated housing can be serviced only with permission under the specific housing provider’s rules; we are not an authorized university vendor merely because a building is near campus.

Parents can send practical support

A family member can buy a Tampa Bay Shine gift card online as a move-in, exam-week or birthday present. Gift cards apply toward eligible services under their purchase terms, and the amount may not cover an entire cleaning.

Student housing property managers

For turnover of multiple apartments or common-area contracts, contact our property-management cleaning team for a separate commercial proposal. Individual student bookings continue using the regular residential checkout.

Frequently asked questions

Do you provide student housing cleaning in Tampa?

Yes. Tampa Bay Shine provides residential cleaning for eligible student apartments in its Tampa-area service area, subject to appointment availability and building access.

Do you clean on-campus dorm rooms?

Only if the university or housing operator allows outside cleaning services and the student has arranged authorized access.

Are university discounts available?

Yes. New eligible students can use their university coupon for $20 off their first cleaning, with a $125 minimum purchase. Use the campus selector above and enter the code during checkout; eligibility may be verified.

Can a parent buy cleaning for a student?

Yes. Parents can purchase an eligible cleaning gift card through our existing online gift-card checkout.

Can I choose recurring cleaning?

Yes. Standard residential booking includes available frequencies and any applicable current frequency discounts.
