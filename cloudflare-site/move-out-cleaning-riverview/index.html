How to Prepare for Your Riverview Move Clean

1

Remove Personal Belongings

Empty or substantially empty properties provide better access to floors, baseboards, closets and detailed cleaning areas.

2

Empty Cabinets & Appliances

Clear drawers, cabinet interiors, refrigerator, freezer and closets where interior cleaning is expected.

3

Keep Utilities On

Keep electricity and water available so bathrooms, appliances, lighting and floors can be properly serviced.

4

Share Access & Deadline Details

Provide gate, guard, parking, garage, lockbox or keypad instructions and your walkthrough, key-return or closing deadline.

Riverview Move Cleaning Questions

Move-Out Cleaning FAQs — Riverview, FL

How much does move-out cleaning cost in Riverview?
Move-In / Move-Out Cleaning starts at $259. Your exact residential price depends on the property and selected options and is shown online before you enter payment information.
Can you coordinate HOA and gated-community access in Riverview?
Yes. Share gate codes, guard-gate instructions, guest-lane procedures, parking limits, garage access, lockbox details and other community requirements when booking.
How long does move-out cleaning take in Riverview?
Timing depends on property size, condition, number of bathrooms, interior detail, selected options and access requirements. The Move-Out Cleaning Cost & Time Guide explains the main planning factors.
Are the oven, refrigerator and cabinet interiors included?
Move-In / Move-Out Cleaning includes inside the oven and refrigerator and empty cabinet and drawer interiors as part of the confirmed move-clean scope. Those areas should be emptied and accessible before service.
What's different about move-out cleaning and Standard Cleaning?
Standard Cleaning is intended for routine upkeep of an occupied home. Move-In / Move-Out Cleaning is designed for an empty or substantially empty property and adds details such as appliance interiors, empty cabinet and drawer interiors, baseboards and closet shelving.
Can you prioritize items from my landlord or property manager?
Yes. Share the landlord or property-manager checklist before service so priorities can be matched to the confirmed cleaning scope. Cleaning does not guarantee return of a security deposit.
Should the property be empty before cleaning?
Move-Out Cleaning is designed for an empty or substantially empty property. Removing furniture and belongings provides better access to floors, baseboards, closets, cabinets and appliances.
Do you bring cleaning supplies?
Yes. Tampa Bay Shine brings cleaning supplies for the scheduled service. Customer-provided products or equipment can also be used when requested.
What if an included area is missed?
Report a qualifying missed area from the original confirmed checklist within 24 hours. Tampa Bay Shine can return to re-clean that specific qualifying area at no additional charge. Permanent stains, damage, conditions outside the cleaner's control and areas affected after service are excluded.
