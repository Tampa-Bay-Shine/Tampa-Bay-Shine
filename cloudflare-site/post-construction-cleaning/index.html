How the Cleaning Process Works

1) Share the Scope

2) Detail the Dust
We clean high-to-low, use repeated passes, and focus on dust-prone edges, ledges, vents, fixtures, and floors.

Post-Construction Cleaning FAQs

Do you use fine-dust filtration?
We use equipment and methods suited to fine construction dust. Specialized remediation, HVAC duct cleaning, or hazardous-material cleanup requires a separate specialist.
Can you handle paint or adhesive residue?
Light residue may be addressed when safe for the surface. Heavy scraping, adhesive removal, or paint removal requires a specialized scope.
Are cabinets and drawers included?
Yes, empty and accessible cabinet and drawer interiors can be included in the post-construction scope.
Do you clean high windows or use ladders?
We focus on reachable areas without ladder work. For glass beyond standard reach, pair the project with a separate window-cleaning quote or specialist scope.
Is post-construction cleaning different from a deep clean?
Yes. Deep cleaning focuses on buildup and a detailed reset. Post-construction cleaning focuses on fine dust, renovation residue, new-finish care, and multi-pass detail after construction or repair work.
