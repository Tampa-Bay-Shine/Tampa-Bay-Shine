Commercial Cleaning FAQs — Tampa, FL

What's included in commercial cleaning?
Commercial cleaning can include lobbies, restrooms, kitchens, workspaces, dusting, floors, trash removal, and high-touch disinfection according to the confirmed scope.
Do you offer after-hours service?
Yes. Night and weekend cleaning can be scheduled to fit business operations and reduce disruption.
Are supplies and equipment included?
Yes. Tampa Bay Shine provides professional cleaning supplies and equipment, and low-VOC options are available on request.
Can you handle multi-site locations?
Yes. Share the locations and required frequency, and Tampa Bay Shine can coordinate a consistent setup and service plan.
Do you clean condo and HOA common areas?
Yes. For lobbies, hallways, elevators, stairwells, mail and package rooms, and amenity spaces, see Condo & HOA Common Area Cleaning . A walkthrough can be used to finalize the scope.
Do you offer window cleaning for commercial spaces?
Yes. Tampa Bay Shine offers Window Cleaning for offices, storefronts, and client-facing spaces, including interior glass, reachable exterior glass, screens, tracks, and sills.
How do you price commercial cleaning?
Pricing depends on facility size, soil level, access requirements, cleaning scope, and service frequency. Request a walkthrough for a tailored quote.
Can you add floor care or window cleaning?
Yes. Floor care, interior windows, and other add-ons can be included in the plan. See Floor Cleaning & Maintenance , Floor Stripping & Waxing , and Window Cleaning for dedicated service information.
