Commercial Cleaning Programs

Routine Service or One-Time Cleanup — Built to Fit.

Commercial cleaning is not one-size-fits-all. Some facilities need recurring janitorial service several times a week; others need a one-time seasonal reset, floor work, windows, post-construction cleanup, or event support.

Recurring Janitorial

Scheduled cleaning for offices, restrooms, breakrooms, shared spaces, high-touch surfaces, floors, trash, and other agreed facility areas. Frequency can be matched to traffic, occupancy, operating hours, and presentation standards.

One-Time & Seasonal Cleaning

One-time commercial cleaning is available for seasonal resets, tenant or property transitions, special projects, inspections, reopening preparation, and spaces that need a deeper cleanup before returning to a recurring maintenance plan.

Specialty Add-Ons

Commercial scopes can include floor cleaning, floor stripping and waxing, interior or reachable exterior windows, post-construction dust removal, post-event cleaning, and other facility-specific work when those services fit the property.

Property Managers & Multi-Site Accounts

One cleaning partner for multiple property needs.

Property managers may need different cleaning scopes across the same portfolio: apartment turns, common-area cleaning, office cleaning, gym and amenity cleaning, floors, windows, or recurring janitorial service. Tampa Bay Shine can build site-specific scopes so each property gets the service it actually needs while access instructions, scheduling, and COI requirements are documented by location.
