When to Choose Deep Cleaning

More Detail Than Routine Maintenance

Deep Cleaning is designed for occupied homes that need a more thorough reset than Standard Cleaning. It adds attention to buildup and detail areas such as baseboards, reachable fans, vents, doors, ledges, fixtures and floor edges.

Starting recurring service afterward?
A Deep Cleaning can establish a cleaner baseline before switching to recurring Standard Cleaning .
Best Uses

When a Deep Clean Makes Sense

First-Time Cleaning

A good starting point when the home has not been professionally cleaned recently or has accumulated dust and buildup.

Seasonal Reset

Refresh baseboards, fans, vents, edges, bathrooms, kitchens and other detail areas.

Before Recurring Service

Establish a cleaner baseline before moving into weekly, every-other-week, every-3-weeks or every-4-weeks maintenance cleaning.

Visible Buildup

Choose Deep Cleaning when routine wiping and floor care are not enough to address accumulated dust and everyday buildup.

Pre-Listing Preparation

Apartment or Condo Reset

Detailed Cleaning Scope

What’s Included in Deep Cleaning

Deep Cleaning includes the core residential cleaning scope plus additional attention to buildup and detail areas. Review our Cleaning Checklist for the complete service comparison.

Kitchen

  • Countertops, sinks and backsplash cleaned
  • Stovetop and appliance exteriors cleaned
  • Inside microwave cleaned
  • Cabinet fronts and handles wiped
  • Doors, switches and touchpoints cleaned
  • Baseboards, edges and reachable ledges detailed
  • Floors vacuumed and mopped

Inside oven, inside refrigerator and inside empty cabinets can be selected separately when available.

Bathrooms

  • Showers, tubs, toilets and sinks cleaned
  • Vanities, mirrors and fixtures cleaned
  • Visible soap buildup and edges addressed
  • Doors, switches and touchpoints wiped
  • Baseboards, vents and corners detailed
  • Floors vacuumed and mopped

Bedrooms

  • Detailed dusting of reachable surfaces
  • Reachable fans and vents dusted
  • Doors, frames and baseboards detailed
  • Bed made when linens are available
  • Carpets vacuumed and hard floors mopped

Living & Common Areas

  • Detailed dusting of reachable furniture and decor
  • Ledges and reachable fixtures dusted
  • Cobwebs removed from reachable areas
  • Doors and baseboards detailed
  • Floor edges and corners addressed
  • Floors vacuumed and mopped

Deep Cleaning Questions

Deep Cleaning FAQs

How much does deep cleaning cost in Tampa?
Tampa Bay Shine Deep Cleaning starts at $199. Your exact price depends on your property size, bedrooms, bathrooms and selected options and is shown online before you enter payment information.
What is the difference between Deep Cleaning and Standard Cleaning?
Standard Cleaning is designed for routine maintenance. Deep Cleaning adds more detailed attention to buildup and areas such as baseboards, reachable fans, vents, doors, fixtures, ledges and floor edges.
When should I book Deep Cleaning?
Deep Cleaning is a strong fit for first-time service, seasonal resets, visible buildup, pre-listing preparation or when the home needs more detail than routine maintenance cleaning.
Do you clean baseboards during a Deep Cleaning?
Yes. Baseboards are part of the additional detail focus of Deep Cleaning, along with reachable fans, vents, doors, ledges, fixtures, floor edges and other buildup areas.
Are inside the oven and refrigerator included?
Inside oven, inside refrigerator and inside empty cabinets are separate selectable services when available. Review the booking options and Cleaning Checklist for the scope of your service.
Should I book Deep Cleaning or Move-Out Cleaning?
Choose Deep Cleaning for an occupied home that needs a detailed reset. Choose Move-In / Move-Out Cleaning for a vacant property where final-handoff and empty-property cleaning details are the priority.
