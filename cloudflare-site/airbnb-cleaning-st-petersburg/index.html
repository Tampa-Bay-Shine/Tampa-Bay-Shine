Host Playbook — Faster, Consistent STR Turnovers

Prep Before Guests Arrive

  • Save a master list for restocking (checklist template).
  • Stage extra linens and toiletries in a labeled cleaner closet.
  • Leave access/parking and Wi-Fi notes in the cleaner app.

After Checkout Routine

  • Collect used linens, assess damage, and triage quick fixes.
  • Reset kitchen & baths, high-touch points, and flooring.
  • Optional photo log for quality control & guest disputes.

Smart Turnover Tips

  • Schedule a periodic deep cleaning every 6–8 weeks.
  • Keep small inventory on hand; request supply coordination.
  • Use recurring calendar holds to protect check-in times.
