By using our website or services, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information in the following ways:

1.1 Information You Provide Directly

When you interact with our website, book services, or communicate with us, you may provide:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Service address
  • Payment information processed securely through third-party providers
  • Details about your home or business for cleaning estimates
  • Messages sent through contact forms, customer portals, email, or SMS
  • Booking and scheduling preferences
  • SMS messaging preferences and consent or opt-in information

1.2 Automatically Collected Information

When you visit our website, we may automatically collect:

  • IP address
  • Browser type and device information
  • Pages visited and time spent on site
  • Cookies and tracking technologies used for analytics and performance
  • Referring site or marketing source

1.3 Information from Third Parties

We may receive information through:

  • BookingKoala or other booking and scheduling platforms
  • Payment processors for billing confirmations and payment status
  • Marketing and advertising platforms such as Google, Facebook, Instagram, and LinkedIn
  • Review platforms such as Google Business Profile and Yelp
  • Communications providers used to facilitate telephone, email, or SMS communications

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and manage cleaning services
  • Process bookings, estimates, and payments
  • Send appointment confirmations, reminders, and service updates
  • Communicate regarding scheduling, service changes, estimates, or customer support
  • Respond to questions and requests
  • Improve our website performance and user experience
  • Send marketing emails, promotional text messages, discounts, or special offers when you have opted in to receive them
  • Verify identity and prevent fraud
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Improve site speed and performance
  • Analyze website traffic
  • Remember your preferences
  • Measure marketing effectiveness

You may disable cookies in your browser settings, but some parts of our site may not function properly if cookies are disabled.

4. Sharing Your Information

We may share information with trusted third-party service providers when necessary to operate our business and provide services to you, such as:

  • BookingKoala or other online scheduling providers
  • Payment processors such as Stripe, Square, or PayPal
  • Email, telephone, and SMS service providers used for customer communications
  • Analytics and advertising tools such as Google Analytics or Google Ads
  • Website hosting, information technology, and security providers
  • Professional advisors or vendors that assist us in operating our business

These third-party service providers may access information only as reasonably necessary to provide services to Tampa Bay Shine and are not authorized by us to use customer information for their own unrelated marketing purposes.

We may also disclose information if required by law, court order, legal process, or when reasonably necessary to protect our rights, property, customers, employees, or safety.

5. SMS Data Sharing

The following provisions specifically apply to information collected in connection with our SMS and text messaging programs:

  • Customer data is not shared with third parties for promotional or marketing purposes.
  • Mobile opt-in information and SMS consent are never shared with anyone for any purpose.
  • Any information sharing that may be described elsewhere in this Privacy Policy excludes mobile opt-in data and SMS consent information.

We may use service providers that facilitate the technical delivery of SMS communications on our behalf. Such providers process information only as necessary to provide communications services to Tampa Bay Shine and are not authorized to use mobile opt-in information or SMS consent for promotional or marketing purposes.

6. SMS Messaging Program Terms and Conditions

Tampa Bay Shine may offer SMS messaging programs for customer service, scheduling, transactional communications, and marketing communications. Participation in our SMS messaging programs is voluntary.

  1. The messaging program may consist of general customer care messaging used to answer questions, provide support, communicate about estimates, appointments, scheduling, service updates, and other customer-service matters, and/or marketing messaging that may include discount codes, special offers, promotions, or information about Tampa Bay Shine products and services, depending on the messaging program or programs you choose to opt in to. We will only send messaging content that you have explicitly signed up to receive through the applicable opt-in process. Messages will be sent from 727-351-1779.
  2. You can cancel the SMS service at any time. Just text STOP to the phone number from which you received messages. After you send the SMS message STOP to us, we will send you an SMS message confirming that you have been unsubscribed. After this, you will no longer receive SMS messages from that messaging program. If you want to join again, you may sign up again through the applicable opt-in process.
  3. If you are experiencing issues with the messaging program, you may reply with the keyword HELP for more assistance, or contact us directly at support@tampabayshine.com or 727-351-1779.
  4. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
  5. Message and data rates may apply for messages sent to you from us and messages sent by you to us. Message frequency will vary based on your communication needs and the messaging program in which you participate. If you have questions about your text messaging or data plan, please contact your wireless provider.
  6. Consent to receive marketing SMS messages is voluntary and is not a condition of purchasing cleaning services from Tampa Bay Shine.
  7. If you have questions regarding privacy, please review the remainder of this Privacy Policy or contact us at support@tampabayshine.com .

7. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including:

  • Encrypted payment processing through secure providers
  • Secure website hosting using HTTPS
  • Access controls and authentication for staff
  • Reasonable system monitoring and security practices

However, no online, electronic, or communications system can be guaranteed to be 100% secure.

8. Data Retention

We retain your information only as long as reasonably necessary to:

  • Provide services and manage ongoing customer relationships
  • Maintain booking and transaction records
  • Maintain records of customer communications and messaging preferences
  • Document SMS consent and opt-out requests where appropriate
  • Comply with accounting, insurance, tax, and legal requirements
  • Improve future customer experiences

You may contact us to request deletion of your personal information, subject to applicable legal, contractual, recordkeeping, fraud prevention, or other legitimate business requirements.

9. Your Rights & Choices

Depending on your location and applicable laws, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Update or correct your information
  • Request deletion of your information
  • Opt out of marketing emails
  • Opt out of SMS communications by replying STOP
  • Disable cookies through your browser settings
  • Request a copy of certain information in a portable format when applicable

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the information in the “Contact Us” section below.

10. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to external websites, including social media pages, payment processors, booking portals, or partner platforms. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or security of third-party websites. Your use of those websites is subject to their own privacy policies and terms.

11. Children’s Privacy

Tampa Bay Shine does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age. If you believe a child has submitted personal information through our website or services, please contact us so that we can take appropriate steps to delete the information.

12. Updates to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated “Last updated” date. We encourage you to periodically review this page for the latest information about our privacy practices.
