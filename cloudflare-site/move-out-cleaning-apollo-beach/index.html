  • Empty cabinets and drawers

  • Remove loose personal items

Apollo Beach Move Cleaning Questions

Move-Out Cleaning FAQs — Apollo Beach, FL

How much does move-out cleaning cost in Apollo Beach?
Move-In / Move-Out Cleaning starts at $259. Your exact residential price depends on the property and selected options and is shown online before you enter payment information.
What is included in Apollo Beach move-out cleaning?
The confirmed Move-In / Move-Out scope can include kitchens, bathrooms, floors, baseboards, door frames, closet shelves, inside the oven and refrigerator, cabinet fronts and empty cabinet and drawer interiors. Optional property details can be added when available.
Does the home need to be empty?
Move-Out Cleaning is designed for an empty or substantially empty property. Cabinets, drawers, closets and appliances should be emptied when their interiors are part of the cleaning scope.
Can you coordinate HOA access and parking?
Yes. Provide gate codes, guard instructions, visitor-parking requirements, elevator procedures or other HOA and community access information before the appointment.
Do you bring cleaning supplies?
Yes. Tampa Bay Shine brings cleaning supplies for the scheduled service. Customer-provided products or equipment can also be used when requested.
Are slider tracks, patios and garages included?
Slider-track detail, patio or balcony sweeping and garage sweeping can be included when those options are part of the confirmed scope and the areas are accessible.
What's the difference between Deep Cleaning and Move-Out Cleaning?
Deep Cleaning is a detailed reset for an occupied home. Move-In / Move-Out Cleaning is designed for an empty or substantially empty property and adds vacant-home details such as appliance interiors, empty cabinet and drawer interiors and closet shelving.
Can move-out cleaning be used before a listing or final walkthrough?
Yes. Apollo Beach Move-In / Move-Out Cleaning can be scheduled for lease turnovers, listing preparation, final walkthroughs, buyer move-ins and post-closing resets.
What if an included area is missed?
Report a qualifying missed area from the original confirmed checklist within 24 hours. Tampa Bay Shine can return to re-clean that specific qualifying area at no additional charge. Permanent stains, damage, conditions outside the cleaner's control and areas affected after service are excluded.
