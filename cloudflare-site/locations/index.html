Service Area Questions

Tampa Bay Cleaning Service Area FAQs

Which Tampa Bay areas do you serve?
Tampa Bay Shine serves Tampa, South Tampa, Brandon, Riverview, Wesley Chapel, Apollo Beach, Ruskin, Sun City Center, Lutz, Temple Terrace, Bradenton and surrounding Tampa Bay communities.
Do you serve areas that do not have their own page on your website?
Yes. A community does not need its own website landing page for Tampa Bay Shine to provide service there. Contact us or use online booking to confirm service availability for your address.
Which cleaning services are available in my area?
Service availability can include Standard Cleaning, Deep Cleaning, Move-In / Move-Out Cleaning, apartment and condo cleaning, Airbnb turnover cleaning, office cleaning, commercial cleaning, common-area cleaning and selected specialty services.
Can I get residential pricing online?
Yes. For eligible residential services, enter your property and service information through online booking to see your exact price before entering payment information.
Do you provide office and commercial cleaning throughout Tampa Bay?
Yes. Tampa Bay Shine provides office and commercial cleaning throughout its active service area. Commercial service is based on the property, cleaning scope, frequency and access requirements. Request a walkthrough so we can review the location and service needs.
Do you provide condo and HOA common-area cleaning?
Yes. Tampa Bay Shine provides recurring cleaning for shared spaces such as lobbies, hallways, elevators, stairwells, mail areas, clubhouses and amenities.
Are you licensed and insured?
Yes. Tampa Bay Shine is licensed and insured, and the cleaning professionals servicing customer properties are background-checked. Certificates of Insurance are available on request for commercial clients.
