What's Included in Bathroom Cleaning

  • Scrub showers, tubs, tile, and glass
  • Clean toilets, bases, and the floor area around fixtures
  • Detail sinks, counters, vanities, cabinet fronts, and faucets
  • Clean mirrors and polish chrome or stainless fixtures
  • Wipe touchpoint surfaces such as handles, switches, and door edges
  • Vacuum and mop bathroom floors, edges, and corners
  • Dust reachable vents, lights, shelves, ledges, and baseboards
  • Remove trash and replace liners when available
  • Use professional cleaning products and tools
  • Add deeper detail through deep cleaning when buildup needs extra time

Bathroom Cleaning FAQs

How long does a bathroom cleaning take?
Most standard bathrooms take 30 to 60 minutes, depending on size, soil level, buildup, fixtures, and whether the bathroom is part of a standard, deep, or move-out cleaning.
Do you bring supplies and equipment?
Yes. Tampa Bay Shine brings professional cleaning products and tools. Tell us during booking if your home, apartment, office, or building has product restrictions.
Can I combine bathroom cleaning with whole-home service?
Yes. Bathroom cleaning is included in our standard cleaning, deep cleaning, apartment cleaning, and move-in or move-out cleaning services.
Do you clean business restrooms?
Yes. We clean office restrooms, commercial restrooms, shared restrooms, event restrooms, and non-clinical restroom areas. COI support is available for commercial clients.
Can bathroom cleaning include deep cleaning details?
Yes. Deep cleaning can include extra detail for baseboards, vents, lights, grout buildup, fixtures, floor edges, cabinet fronts, and other high-detail bathroom areas.
