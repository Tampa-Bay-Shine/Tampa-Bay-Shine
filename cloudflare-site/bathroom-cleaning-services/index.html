Typical restroom cleaning tasks

FREQUENT QUESTIONS

Restroom cleaning FAQs

Can you provide restroom attendants during an event?

Restroom attendant coverage can be arranged for receptions, corporate gatherings and other events, subject to staffing, venue access and the agreed schedule.

Do you offer daytime restroom cleaning for busy offices?

Yes. Scheduled daytime restroom checks and touchpoint cleaning can be scoped for office buildings and other busy facilities. Frequency and coverage hours are confirmed in the proposal.

Can restroom cleaning be scheduled at night?

Yes. Nightly or after-hours restroom cleaning can be quoted for offices, facilities and shared spaces based on access and scheduling availability.

Can you replenish toilet paper, soap and other consumables?

Restocking client-supplied toilet paper, hand towels, soap and liners can be included in the agreed service scope. Supply purchasing and billing arrangements are confirmed separately.

How do you price event or ongoing restroom service?

Pricing depends on the number of restrooms and fixtures, occupancy, required coverage hours, visit frequency, restroom condition, access and the agreed supply responsibilities. Request a customized quote.

Do you provide a certificate of insurance?

A certificate of insurance can be provided on request when needed for a qualified commercial account or event venue.
