Quick answer

Students in eligible off-campus apartments near the University of South Florida in Tampa and Temple Terrace can book standard, deep or move-out cleaning with Tampa Bay Shine. Routine and one-time options are available through the same residential booking process; campus residence halls require housing approval.

Apartment life around the USF Tampa campus

The area around USF’s Tampa campus includes student apartments and residential neighborhoods in North Tampa and Temple Terrace. Our student apartment cleaning is designed for qualifying homes in these areas, not for the university’s classrooms or academic facilities. Please confirm your specific address is served before booking.

Shared kitchens and bathrooms need a plan

With roommates, the kitchen sink, counters, stovetop, floors and shared bathrooms see frequent use. Booking periodic standard cleaning helps support a comfortable shared environment; a deep cleaning may be appropriate if grease, soap residue or buildup has been left unattended.

Cleaning around exams and changing semesters

Many students prefer a visit before family comes to town, after an intensive exam period, at move-in, or near a lease-end date. Coordinate roommates and building access early, especially when move-out dates cluster around semester changes.

Move-out checklist before keys are returned

Review your USF-area apartment lease and any instructions from the property manager. Remove personal items and trash, confirm which rooms are included, and allow time before the final inspection. Cleaning does not guarantee return of a security deposit.

Parents and students can coordinate from anywhere

A parent can purchase a cleaning gift card while the student manages the appointment and authorized entry. The existing gift-card checkout provides the purchase flow; the student can use it for an eligible service.
