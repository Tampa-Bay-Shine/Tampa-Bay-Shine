Quick answer

Parents and relatives can purchase a Tampa Bay Shine cleaning gift card online for students with eligible Tampa-area residential addresses. Students choose a covered service and coordinate their own authorized access and appointment; the gift-card amount may not cover the full cleaning price.

A practical gift for students near USF and UTampa

Students juggling coursework, roommates and part-time jobs may appreciate help with their apartment. Our cleaning gift cards can be used toward eligible residential services at serviced addresses near USF, the University of Tampa and other Tampa-area campuses.

When a cleaning gift makes sense

Consider a gift around move-in, finals, a birthday, a difficult semester or the weeks before graduation and move-out. There is no need to estimate the whole cleaning price in advance if you are gifting a contribution rather than promising a complete service.

How to purchase and use a gift card

Use our existing secure gift-card purchase page. The student can check which cleaning service fits their apartment and coordinate scheduling and building access. Purchase and redemption follow the current gift-card terms.

Know what the gift covers

Pricing varies with the service and apartment details. Gift cards hold value toward eligible services; they do not guarantee a full cleaning or override property access restrictions.

On-campus housing restrictions still apply

Even if a student lives in university-owned housing, an outside cleaning service may be restricted. Verify the housing operator’s third-party provider policy before making a plan.

Frequently asked questions

Can I buy a cleaning gift for a USF or University of Tampa student?

Yes, when the student’s residential address and the selected service are within our coverage and eligibility rules.

Can the gift card pay for an entire cleaning?

It depends on its balance and the selected cleaning price. You may be contributing toward rather than covering the full appointment.

Can a parent schedule on behalf of a student?

A parent may help coordinate, but authorized building access and the customer’s consent must be arranged.

Is there a discount on gift-card purchases?

Do not assume first-clean coupons apply to gift-card purchases; verify the current gift-card and promotion terms.
