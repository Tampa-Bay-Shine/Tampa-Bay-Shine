Commercial Cleaning Offer FAQs

Who qualifies for the free first commercial cleaning?
Qualified new commercial clients may be eligible after a quote or walkthrough. Eligibility depends on service area, cleaning frequency, scope, facility type, building access, scheduling, and the approved recurring cleaning plan.
Is this offer for one-time cleanings?
The offer is primarily intended for qualified new recurring commercial cleaning clients. Some one-time or specialty projects may not qualify.
What types of businesses do you clean?
Tampa Bay Shine cleans offices, professional suites, medical and dental offices, gyms, retail spaces, common areas, property management spaces, and other approved commercial locations.
Do you provide after-hours cleaning?
Commercial cleaning may be available during business hours, after hours, or on a custom schedule depending on the location, access requirements, and current availability.
Do you provide floor stripping or specialty floor care?
Routine vacuuming, sweeping, and mopping may be included in a commercial cleaning scope. Specialty floor care, stripping, waxing, polishing, or project-based floor work requires a separate quote.
