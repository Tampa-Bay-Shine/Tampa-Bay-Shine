Before the Appointment

How to Prepare for Move-Out Cleaning

1

Empty the Property

Remove belongings whenever possible so floors, baseboards, closets and other surfaces can be reached.

2

Empty Interior Areas

Empty cabinets, drawers, closets, refrigerator and freezer when those interiors are expected to be cleaned.

3

Keep Utilities On

Keep water and electricity available so bathrooms, appliances, lighting and floors can be properly serviced.

4

Share Access & Deadlines

Provide gate, concierge, parking, elevator, lockbox or keypad details and tell us about inspection, closing or walkthrough timing.

Move-Out Cleaning Questions

Tampa Bay Move-Out Cleaning FAQs

Is this the move-out cleaning page for Tampa?
This is Tampa Bay Shine's regional move-out cleaning hub. For Tampa, Downtown Tampa or South Tampa-specific information, visit the dedicated Move-Out Cleaning in Tampa page.
How much does move-out cleaning cost?
Move-In / Move-Out Cleaning starts at $259. Your exact residential price depends on the property and selected options and is shown online before you enter payment information.
What areas have dedicated move-out cleaning pages?
Tampa Bay Shine has dedicated move-out cleaning pages for Tampa, Brandon, Riverview, Apollo Beach and Wesley Chapel. Additional service-area information is available on the Service Areas page.
Are the refrigerator, oven and cabinet interiors included?
Move-In / Move-Out Cleaning includes inside the oven and refrigerator and empty cabinet and drawer interiors as part of the confirmed move-clean scope. The property should be empty or substantially empty and those interior areas should be cleared.
Can you clean apartments and condos during a move-out?
Yes. Tampa Bay Shine provides move-out cleaning for apartments and condos and can work with parking, gates, concierge desks, elevators, leasing offices, lockboxes and other access instructions when applicable.
Does the property need to be completely empty?
Move-Out Cleaning is designed for an empty or substantially empty property. Removing furniture and belongings generally provides better access to floors, baseboards, closets, cabinets and other move-clean areas.
Do you bring cleaning supplies?
Yes. Tampa Bay Shine brings cleaning supplies for the scheduled service. Customer-provided products or equipment can also be used when requested.
What's the difference between Deep Cleaning and Move-Out Cleaning?
Deep Cleaning is a detailed reset for an occupied home. Move-In / Move-Out Cleaning is designed for an empty or substantially empty property and adds vacant-home details such as appliance interiors, empty cabinet and drawer interiors and closet shelving.
What if an included area is missed?
Report a qualifying missed area from the original confirmed checklist within 24 hours. Tampa Bay Shine can return to re-clean that specific qualifying area at no additional charge. Permanent stains, damage, conditions outside the cleaner's control and areas affected after service are excluded.
