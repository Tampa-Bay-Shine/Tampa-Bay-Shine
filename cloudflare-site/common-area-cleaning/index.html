Common Area Cleaning FAQs

What is included in condo association common area cleaning?
Common-area cleaning can include lobbies, hallways, elevators, stairwells, mailrooms, clubhouses, fitness rooms, common-area restrooms, visible glass, and other shared building spaces. Final scope depends on the property layout and service frequency.
Do you work with HOA boards and property managers?
Yes. Tampa Bay Shine builds cleaning scopes for HOA boards, condo associations, apartment communities, and property managers that need recurring service for shared spaces.
Do you offer recurring janitorial service for condo buildings?
Yes. Communities can choose recurring janitorial service multiple times per week, with porter coverage or a weekly detail day for higher-traffic properties.
Can you clean clubhouses and amenity spaces?
Yes. Clubhouses, lounges, fitness rooms, package rooms, mailrooms, and amenity restrooms can be included in the recurring common-area scope.
Can you provide a COI naming the association or management company?
Yes. COIs are available on request and can name the HOA, condo association, building owner, or management company as needed.
Do you clean after hours or during quiet hours?
Yes. After-hours schedules are available for condo buildings and shared spaces, with service coordinated around quiet-hour and access requirements when needed.
Do you handle lobby, hallway, and elevator cleaning?
Yes. Those are core parts of many common-area cleaning programs, along with stairwells, restrooms, mail areas, package rooms, and clubhouses.
How is pricing determined for common-area cleaning?
Pricing depends on the square footage of shared areas, traffic level, floor type, number of restrooms and amenities, and the required service frequency.
