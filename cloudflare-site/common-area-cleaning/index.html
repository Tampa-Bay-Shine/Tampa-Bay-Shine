Good to know

Frequently asked questions

What is included in condo association common area cleaning?

Common-area cleaning can include lobbies, hallways, elevators, stairwells, mailrooms, clubhouses, fitness rooms, shared restrooms and visible glass. The final tasks depend on your layout and service frequency.

Do you work with HOA boards and property managers?

Yes. We develop recurring scopes for HOA boards, condo associations, apartment communities and property-management teams.

Can you provide a COI for our association?

Certificates of insurance are available on request. Tell us your association or management company's requirements during the walkthrough.

Can cleaning happen after hours?

After-hours options may be available, coordinated with building access and quiet-hour requirements.

How is common-area cleaning priced?

Pricing depends on the shared-space square footage, traffic, floors, restrooms, amenities and requested frequency. We prepare a customized quote after reviewing your property.
