Checklist Questions

Move-Out Cleaning Checklist FAQs

What should I clean first before a move-out walkthrough?
Start with appliances, cabinets, drawers, bathrooms, closets and other interior details. Save floors for last so the property looks fresh when the walkthrough begins.
Does the home need to be empty for move-out cleaning?
Move-out cleaning works best when the property is empty or substantially empty. Cabinet interiors, appliance interiors, closets, baseboards and floors are easier to access after personal belongings have been removed.
Should utilities stay on for move-out cleaning?
Yes. Keep water and electricity on until cleaning is complete so appliance interiors, bathrooms, sinks and floors can be cleaned properly.
Should I follow my landlord's checklist or this checklist?
Follow any checklist supplied by your landlord, leasing office, HOA, buyer or property manager first. Use this page as a broader room-by-room planning guide.
Are cabinet and drawer interiors easier to clean when empty?
Yes. Remove belongings before cleaning cabinet and drawer interiors so the surfaces can be properly wiped and inspected.
