Questions

Tampa Bay Shine Cleaning FAQs

How much does house cleaning start at?
Standard Cleaning starts at $114 for the lowest one-time configuration: an under-1,000-sq-ft, 1-bedroom, 1-bathroom home. Deep Cleaning starts at $199, and Move-In / Move-Out Cleaning starts at $259. Your exact price is calculated during online booking before you enter payment information.
How does the RECUR30 offer work?
RECUR30 gives new customers 30% off the first cleaning when starting a recurring weekly, every-other-week, every-3-weeks, or every-4-weeks plan. A minimum $100 service value is required before discount. The customer must complete 3 total cleanings: the discounted first cleaning plus 2 additional recurring cleanings. RECUR30 does not stack with the normal recurring frequency discount on the first visit. If service is canceled before the 3 total cleanings are completed, the original 30% introductory discount is charged back.
What if I only need a one-time cleaning?
New customers who prefer a one-time cleaning can use code SUMMER for $25 off a cleaning with a minimum $100 service value before discount. SUMMER does not require a recurring commitment.
Do I see my exact price before entering payment information?
Yes. Online booking shows your exact service price before payment information is entered. An account is created to make repeat and recurring bookings easier and to allow address, payment, service-change, and cancellation requests.
What does the 24-hour satisfaction guarantee cover?
If an area included in the original service checklist was missed or not cleaned to expectations, report it within 24 hours of service completion and Tampa Bay Shine will return to re-clean that specific area at no additional cost. The guarantee does not apply to conditions outside our control such as permanent stains, severe damage, permanent residue, or situations where third parties entered the property after our visit.
What areas does Tampa Bay Shine serve?
Tampa Bay Shine serves Tampa, South Tampa, Brandon, Riverview, Wesley Chapel, Apollo Beach, Ruskin, Sun City Center, Lutz, Temple Terrace, Bradenton, and communities between our core service areas. Availability can vary by route and schedule.
Do you provide recurring office and commercial cleaning?
Yes. Tampa Bay Shine provides recurring office and commercial cleaning, including professional offices, commercial facilities, non-clinical medical-office administrative areas, and condo or HOA common areas. Commercial customers can request a walkthrough through the contact page.
