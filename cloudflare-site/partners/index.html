Partner FAQs

Are these companies owned by Tampa Bay Shine?
No. Featured partners are independent businesses. Tampa Bay Shine lists them as helpful local resources, but each partner manages its own scheduling, pricing, work quality, licenses, warranties, and customer service.
How do I contact a listed partner?
Use the phone, email, or website listed on the partner card. Contact the partner directly for availability, estimates, service details, and appointment scheduling.
Can Tampa Bay Shine coordinate cleaning around partner work?
Yes. For cleaning services, we can help schedule around move-outs, move-ins, listings, repairs, pool service, garage door work, or other property-care appointments when you provide the timeline.
How can a business become a featured partner?
Contact Tampa Bay Shine with your company name, service category, service areas, website, phone number, email, and why your business is a good fit for local homeowners, Realtors, landlords, or property managers.
Can partners link to Tampa Bay Shine?
Yes, when it genuinely helps your customers. Partners can link to the most relevant Tampa Bay Shine page, such as house cleaning, move-out cleaning, commercial cleaning, office cleaning, common area cleaning, or service areas.
Do you recommend partners for property managers and Realtors?
Yes. Partners can be useful for property managers, landlords, Realtors, and homeowners who need related property services before or after cleaning, listings, turnovers, showings, or final walkthroughs.
