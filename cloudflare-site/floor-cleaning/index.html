What’s Included in Floor Cleaning

  • Routine neutral cleaning for resilient and sealed floors
  • Machine scrubbing based on soil level and traffic
  • Burnishing programs to improve gloss and appearance
  • Traffic-lane touchups and presentation-focused detailing
  • Floor care recommendations based on finish wear and use
  • VCT, LVT, sheet vinyl, and similar resilient surfaces
  • Sealed concrete and epoxy in appropriate environments
  • Tile floor maintenance
  • Rubber and sports flooring where applicable
  • COI and product documentation on request

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer one-time floor cleaning and recurring programs?

Yes. We handle both one-time floor cleaning and recurring maintenance programs depending on the facility, traffic, and finish condition.

What’s the difference between floor cleaning and floor stripping & waxing?

Floor cleaning focuses on maintenance such as scrubbing, neutral cleaning, and burnishing. Floor Stripping & Waxing is a heavier restoration process used when finish layers are too worn to maintain effectively.

What floor types do you service?

We service common commercial floor types such as VCT, LVT, sheet vinyl, sealed concrete, tile, and other maintenance-friendly surfaces based on site conditions.

Can floor care be combined with janitorial or window cleaning?

Yes. Many clients combine Office Cleaning or Commercial Cleaning with Window Cleaning and floor maintenance for a cleaner overall presentation.
