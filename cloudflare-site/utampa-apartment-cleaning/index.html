Quick answer

Tampa Bay Shine cleans eligible off-campus apartments near the University of Tampa in Downtown and South Tampa. Students can choose standard or deep cleaning for occupied apartments or move-out service for a lease ending, subject to normal pricing, coverage and building access rules.

Apartment cleaning close to Plant Hall and downtown

The University of Tampa sits near the Hillsborough River and downtown Tampa, with students renting in nearby central-city neighborhoods. Apartment buildings in these areas may have controlled entry, elevators and parking procedures, so provide relevant access instructions when booking.

City apartment living and busy schedules

Students combining classes, internships, work and campus activities may want a practical solution for routine dusting, floors, kitchens and bathrooms. Standard cleaning is ideal for maintained homes; deep cleaning addresses more detailed needs when the apartment requires extra attention.

Moving between leases in Downtown or South Tampa

For lease-end appointments, plan around elevator reservations, move-out windows and final key drop-off. Remove your belongings and coordinate cleaning with roommates. A move-out clean can prepare a unit for its next occupant but does not promise a deposit refund.

A useful gift for a UTampa student
